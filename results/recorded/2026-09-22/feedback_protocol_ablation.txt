==============================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS
==============================================================================
  'retry' carries no information, so any condition that fails to beat it
  is not being helped by the verifier's message.
  generic vs retry        -> does being told 'wrong' matter at all?
  diagnostic vs generic   -> does the verifier's CONTENT matter?
  actionable vs diagnostic-> does phrasing it as a procedure matter?
  constrained vs actionable-> does protecting the answer format recover
                             the loss caused by format degradation?
  If 'format ok' is much lower for the feedback conditions, the accuracy
  comparison is still partly measuring formatting, not reasoning.
