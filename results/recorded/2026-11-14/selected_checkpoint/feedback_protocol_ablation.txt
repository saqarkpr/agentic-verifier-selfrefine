HOW TO READ THIS
==============================================================================

  generic vs retry        -> does being told 'wrong' matter at all?
  diagnostic vs generic   -> does the verifier's CONTENT matter?
  actionable vs diagnostic-> does phrasing it as a procedure matter?
  constrained vs actionable-> does protecting the answer format recover
